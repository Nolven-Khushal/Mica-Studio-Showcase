# Everything Mica Studio does

A complete tour of the product, screen by screen. (Screens shown in the video use made-up demo data.)

## Home
- **The day at a glance:** what is late to dispatch, what is overdue to collect, what is due to pay this week, sales today.
- **Period tiles:** sales, gross profit, net profit after expenses, purchases, stock on hand, money to receive, money to pay, each with a small trend line. This month, last month, last 90 days, this financial year, or a custom range.
- **A setup checklist** that ticks itself as the gallery adopts the app (company details, bank details, recovery key, backups, first invoices).
- **Light and dark** themes, a **notification centre**, and **search everything** (invoices, customers, items) with one shortcut.

## Buying
- **Purchase invoices** for three verticals (Plywood, WPC, Mica), each with the line columns its paper invoice has. One vertical per purchase invoice.
- **Fast entry:** copy lines from the supplier's previous invoice, remembered terms, GST and tax type picked automatically, duplicate invoice numbers refused, a wrong total flagged.
- **Paper bill attached** to the invoice, and a side panel that opens any invoice without leaving the list.
- **Import from a supplier's Excel register**, including discount columns, and credit memos matched to the invoices they correct.
- **Purchase orders:** pick what you need, send the order to the supplier's WhatsApp group in one go; it shows as received when the invoice is entered.
- **"Sold but not bought yet"** card on a new purchase invoice: every waiting item with its quantity, added in one click.

## Selling
- **Sales invoices** with one vertical or several on the same bill; saved customer or walk-in; GST type from the GSTIN.
- **Pick the exact batch** for every line, so cost and profit are exact. The selling price fills from the rate sheet and any change is flagged to the owner.
- **Order first (not in stock):** sell before the goods arrive. The line waits, shows as negative stock, and fills by itself, oldest sale first, when the purchase invoice is entered.
- **Freight loading / unloading** as a separate no-GST charge, shown as income.
- **Official GST tax invoice** export, PDF and WhatsApp, with the paper bill attached.
- **Sales list** with profit, dispatch and payment status per invoice, filters, an Excel-style lines view, saved views.

## Dispatch and delivery
- An invoice should leave within **three days**; late ones turn red.
- **Part dispatch:** send some now, the rest later; on hand and yet-to-dispatch stay exact.
- **Delivery with wastage:** pieces broken on the road are booked as a gallery expense and come off that bill's profit.
- **Board view:** cards for To dispatch, In transit, Delivered.

## Notes
- **Credit and debit notes** against sales or purchase invoices: returns, price corrections, extra charges. Each moves stock and money together, with the GST right.
- Money-only notes (no goods) for supplier credits and rate differences.

## Stock
- **Stock is a ledger of movements** (purchase in, sale out, notes, adjustment); on hand is the total.
- **By item, by batch, yet to dispatch, awaiting purchase,** with cost value, age and last sale.
- **History of every batch** with the reason on each line.
- **Owner-only stock adjustment**, and a bulk **stock reset** step that never touches purchases, sales, payables, receivables or profit.
- **Inventory report** in Excel.

## Money
- **Due dates:** money to receive and to pay, by due date, with an **ageing** chart.
- **Receipts and payments:** one entry can go on several bills (part payments included). Whatever does not fit a bill stays **on account** as an advance and shows on the party's ledger.
- **Earlier balance:** advances that were really for bills from before the system are booked against an opening entry, so the ledger reads nil while due dates and overdue figures do not move.
- **Payment reminders:** friendly, firm or final, in **English, Hindi and Gujarati**; opened in WhatsApp, never sent by itself.
- **Gallery expenses:** rent, salary, electricity, transport; wastage and freight arrive automatically.
- **Party ledgers** (customers and suppliers): debit, credit, running balance, exports to PDF and Excel.
- **Journal (owner only):** opening balances, transfers, contra between a customer and a supplier, discounts and write-offs, adjustments.

## Profit, products and reports
- **Profit and costing** by invoice, item, customer, month, vertical, B2B against B2C, with wastage and expenses.
- **Products and rates:** the rate sheet as rules, price groups for foils, "price to confirm" flags, a price check, margin per item, minimum stock.
- **Reports:** finance, sales, inventory, costing and profit, and a one-page **daily summary**, each in Excel and PDF; live Excel registers kept up to date automatically; key insights read from sales, purchases, stock and dispatch together.

## Safety and control
- **Login with roles:** the owner sees everything; staff can enter data but cannot change, cancel or open the journal, backups or sharing.
- **Encrypted backups** after every change, verified, pruned, with a **recovery key**; an owner's laptop keeps its own encrypted copy.
- **Automatic data check** of the books (stock, totals, ledgers against due dates, numbering) at every start and on demand.
- **Automatic restore:** a damaged database is set aside and the newest good backup restores itself; the app never starts empty by itself.
- **Safe database upgrades:** a copy of the old file first.
- **Corrections files:** an ordered list of exact fixes, previewed, backed up first, each step in its own transaction, safe to apply twice.

## Two PCs, one set of books
- One **main PC** holds the single live database; other computers open the same program from it. No merging, no sync conflicts, nothing stored on the second machine.
- **Private by design:** HTTPS with a pinned certificate, private addresses only, one-time pairing codes, no cloud server.
- **Safe retries:** a save pressed twice after a lost reply creates one invoice.
- A **lock screen**, a connection monitor that locks the screen when the main PC cannot be reached, and live refresh when somebody else saves.

## Look and feel
- Frosted-glass dark and light themes in the brand's orange, white and black.
- A top menu with panels, a side panel for invoices, table density switch, keyboard shortcuts, motion that stays out of the way and switches off for reduced motion.
